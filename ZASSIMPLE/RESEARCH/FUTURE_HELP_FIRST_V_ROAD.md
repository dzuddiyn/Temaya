**Status:** OPEN FUTURE WORK — NOT LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-10-08  
**Scope:** Future commercial/product direction only. Not current Stage 0/1 implementation scope.

## Source lineage

This future direction intentionally adapts the **Kerani_Core_SuperBasic D-048 — V-Road / 7V Core + Extended V-Gates** philosophy.

Kerani canonical 7V Core:

```text
Value → Verbal → Volume → Viral → Vary → Venture → Versatile
```

Key evidence discipline:
- **No V is free. Every V must be earned.**
- **Do not skip the Vs.**
- Value means solving a real problem before chasing reach, signup count, feature count or market size.
- Verbal means meeting users through simple natural interaction while keeping structure/authority deterministic underneath.
- Volume and Viral are earned only after repeated usefulness, onboarding, reliability, cost controls and truthful failure handling are proven.

This note does **not** import Kerani's micro-SME domain assumptions into Temaya. It adapts only the value-first growth/maturity philosophy.

---

## Owner future aspiration

Temaya commercialisation should be **help-first, not market-first**.

The product should not begin by asking:
- "Which market is biggest?"
- "Which demographic can pay most?"
- "Which segment gives the fastest growth?"

It should begin by asking:
- "What recurring human problem can Temaya genuinely reduce?"
- "Can it help reliably in real daily life?"
- "Does it reduce friction, loneliness, cognitive burden or coordination burden?"
- "Can it do so while preserving privacy, dignity, user control and trust?"

Market formation should follow demonstrated usefulness rather than lead it.

## Practical Malaysia view

The owner does **not** assume every Malaysian business will have its own AI agent.

A more realistic future is sufficient:

- major digital platforms such as food-delivery, mobility, travel, commerce, banking and service platforms may expose agent-ready front doors;
- Temaya only needs enough interoperable reach to handle useful targeted tasks;
- broad merchant-by-merchant integration is unnecessary if a smaller number of high-coverage digital platforms expose suitable agent/API surfaces.

Illustrative future use:

```text
Temaya
  ↓
scoped request
  ↓
Office / Liaison Agent
  ↓
Food-delivery / mobility / booking platform agent
  ↓
options / availability / quote
  ↓
user chooses or approves
  ↓
transaction
  ↓
verified result / receipt
```

The expected near-term Malaysian trust model is conservative:
- users may accept AI assistance sooner than AI decision-making;
- users may prefer AI to narrow options, prepare actions and remove repetitive steps;
- users may still want to make the final selection themselves;
- targeted automation such as routine food ordering may become acceptable earlier than broad autonomous purchasing.

This is a working product hypothesis, not a factual prediction that must be true.

## Private principal-agent thesis

Temaya's long-term value should remain concentrated in the **private personal/family agent**, not in duplicating the operational front door of every external service.

High-value core:

```text
PRIVATE TEMAYA
├── identity
├── private human memory
├── family context
├── preferences
├── routines
├── accessibility/usability context
├── permissions
├── continuity
└── trust
```

External execution is delegated using the minimum necessary information.

A future Office / Liaison Agent may contact external service agents, but it should not receive unrestricted raw private memory.

## Assistive / social-value direction

Future Temaya should also explore problems where commercial market size is not the first criterion.

Potential areas include:
- older adults living alone;
- people with limited nearby family support;
- users who struggle with daily coordination, reminders, forms, appointments or digital services;
- users who benefit from a persistent companion presence;
- people who may benefit from supportive routine, companionship and practical assistance.

Artificial Soul may become relevant here because continuity, warmth and familiarity can matter alongside task execution.

Important boundary:
- Temaya should support and assist;
- it must not present itself as a replacement for family, community, carers, clinicians, emergency services or other human support where those are needed;
- vulnerable users must not be exploited through dependency-oriented product design.

The purpose is to **reduce burden and help**, not to manufacture engagement.

## Adapted Temaya V-Road

Candidate future commercial maturity path:

### 1. Value
Prove real usefulness inside the owner's own family first.

Evidence:
- repeated real tasks solved;
- lower friction;
- reliable recall/action;
- useful family acceptance;
- safe failure behaviour.

### 2. Verbal
Make Temaya usable through natural familiar surfaces:
- WhatsApp;
- smart speaker / voice;
- other simple channels only where useful.

The backend can be rigorous while the surface remains simple.

### 3. Volume
Only after family reliability:
- test with more real households;
- measure repeated usefulness and retention;
- learn onboarding problems;
- learn privacy/support burden.

Do not count installs/signups as sufficient proof.

### 4. Viral
Prepare for growth before inviting growth:
- onboarding;
- recovery;
- cost limits;
- observability;
- privacy isolation;
- support model;
- graceful degradation;
- truthful status/failure.

### 5. Vary
Support different households, channels, languages, routines, service providers and accessibility needs without forking the core.

### 6. Venture
Use partners where they genuinely accelerate usefulness:
- service-platform integrations;
- public/community programmes;
- infrastructure/payment providers;
- associations;
- support organisations.

Partnership must not surrender user/private-data authority.

### 7. Versatile
Only claim generality after Temaya proves reuse across multiple household contexts, channels and external service ecosystems.

## Extended V-Gates especially important for Temaya

From Kerani D-048, the following gates are especially relevant to a private family AI:

- **Vision** — clear problem/user/outcome.
- **Vibe** — serious backend, simple human experience.
- **Veracity** — no false success/status.
- **Validation** — interpreted intent does not automatically become authoritative action.
- **Verification** — important actions have evidence/read-back.
- **Volition** — user can confirm, correct, cancel, consent and choose.
- **Vault** — private memory, credentials and tenant boundaries protected.
- **Vulnerability** — actively test leakage, duplicate writes, abuse and failure.
- **Velocity** — minimise time to useful value, not merely API latency.
- **Viability** — AI/channel/infrastructure/support economics sustainable.
- **Vigilance** — operational reliability and cost signals monitored.
- **Vendor-neutral** — service/provider replacement should not require rewriting Temaya's core authority model.
- **Value-chain** — Temaya may connect family/users to external services without pretending to own those external Sources of Truth.
